✎About me

I share journaling and stationery content, including spread ideas, product reviews, cozy "journal with me" videos, and lots of funny, relatable posts. I keep it real, soft, and useful. My audience is primarily women (94.9% on Instagram and 78% on TikTok) aged 18 to 34, mostly in the US, UK, India, and Brazil. I’ve partnered with brands such as Paperwrld, Journalsay, Musso EU, Menmin Made, IEEBEE Washi Tape, and Shuttle Art, creating different kinds of content for each. My most recent collaboration was with Menmin Made, and we are currently running a partnership ad!
